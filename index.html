The Future of Artificial Intelligence

September 20, 2025 by Javari Whilby

What's coming by 2034?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has come a long way from Turing’s theoretical thinking machines to today’s multimodal models that can process text, images, audio, and more.
Innovations in deep learning, natural language processing, and generative AI have laid the foundation for a smarter, more accessible future.

Generative AI is shifting from massive, resource-heavy models to smaller, more efficient alternatives. Companies like OpenAI and Meta are driving this trend with models like GPT-4o-mini.
These compact models offer enterprise-grade capabilities while reducing costs and energy demands.
Open-source models like LLaMA 3.1 and Mistral Large are fostering innovation through community-driven development. As prompt engineering matures, AI becomes better at understanding nuanced human input, powering tailored applications in healthcare, finance, education, and more.

Edge Computing

September 21, 2024, by Javari Whilby

Edge computing is transforming how date is processed by bringing computation and storage closer to where data is generated at the edge of the network.
Unlinke traditional cloud computing, which relies on centralized data centers, edge computing reduces latency and bandwith usage by handling data locally. Thius is especially important for time-sensitive applications like autonomous vehicles, remote healthcare, and industrial IoT Systems, where real time processing is crucial.
As 5G Networks expand, edge computing is becoming more viable, enabling faster response times and more reliable connectins for smart devices and apps.
